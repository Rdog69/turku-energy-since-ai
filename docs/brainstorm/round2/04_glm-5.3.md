# Round 2 — red team / contrarian

## 1. Thesis

The round-1 core is right for September and wrong for October in one specific, measurable way: **every adaptive component (recency weights, last-3-weeks isotonic, Sep-residual conformal) is fitted on the wildest, most up-heavy month in the data, while the test month is calmer and more down-heavy on every observable we can already see** — spot std 58.5 → 19.0, spot max 399.6 → 104.2 (`data_samples.md` §5), up share 33.3 % (Sep) → 25.0 % (Oct 1–4, §4 direction table). That mis-calibration lands on 50 of the 80 automatic points (pinball 20 + spike Brier 20 + direction Brier 10) — exactly the columns where the field is weakest (baseline coverage 0.68 vs the 0.80 target). We go from tied to first not with a better classifier but by making **calibration and base rates conditional on context we legitimately know at each origin** (net position, spot level/ramp, wind forecast — all published D-1 and verified usable for all 40 steps, `PLAN.md` finding 10), and by validating on a fold whose target month is *calmer* than its training months.

## 2. Shared validation protocol (all go/no-gos below use it)

- **Fold A** (organiser window): train ≤ 6 Sep → score 7 Sep–4 Oct.
- **Fold B** (calm-target — the October dress rehearsal): train Jun + Aug + Sep, **drop July** → score July. The only fold with the test's geometry: target calmer and more down-heavy than training.
- **Fold C** (stress, keep from round 1): train Jun–Jul → score Aug/Sep.
- **Fold D** (context-matched subset): from A/B/C validation rows keep quarters whose day's (spot-vol tercile, net-position quintile, wind-forecast tercile) matches Oct 1–4.
- **Adopt** a component iff it wins fold A by ≥ 1 % (≥ 2 % spike Brier), loses fold B and fold D by < 0.5 %, and is sign-stable under day-block bootstrap (1000 resamples, ≥ 80 %). Purge 10 h at fold boundaries; every feature through `rows_available_at()`.
- Never decide anything on fold A alone — that is how the round-1 plan tunes itself into September.

## 3. Top 5 ideas, ranked by expected points per hour

### 1. Regime-conditional calibration layer (frozen second-stage model)
- **What:** replace "isotonic on the last 3 Sep weeks" with g(raw probability, step band, **context**) fitted on OOF *training* predictions, where context = net position (consumption − wind − solar forecasts), spot level, trailing spot vol, wind-fc level, hour. For quantiles use **multiplicative widths**: `p10 = p50 − s_lo·(p50 − p10_raw)`, `p90 = p50 + s_hi·(p90_raw − p50)` — order-preserving by construction and self-narrowing in a calm month; fit `s_lo/s_hi` per band × context bin to minimise OOF pinball.
- **Evidence:** October differs on observables (§4/§5 tables above); net position separates up share 21 → 49 % and high-spike share 3 → 14 % across quintiles (round-2 facts); spike shares drifted 9.0/7.3 % → 12.1/13.0 % *within* training (`PLAN.md` finding 9) — calibration drift is demonstrated, not hypothetical.
- **Metrics:** spike Brier −5–10 %, pinball −3–6 %, direction Brier up to ~−0.013 (predicting Sep's 0.33 up share against a true 0.25 costs � 0.388 vs 0.375 optimal); also moves MAE ~1–3 €/MWh because π shifts the mixture median by ~10 €/MWh in down quarters. Confidence medium-high.
- **Cost:** 3–4 h. **Leak traps:** context features must be usable at the origin (spot and DA forecasts are, D-1 13:00/14:00, `availability.csv`); trailing spot-vol from *published* spot only; g fitted on OOF training predictions, never refit at test origins (sidesteps the `PLAN.md` §12 grey question entirely).
- **Go/no-go:** beats Sep-fitted calibration on folds A and D, no loss on B; and check 2 below must show Sep's calibration map is regime-specific.

### 2. Analog-ensemble CDF as the hedge, blended per column
- **What:** k-NN (k ≈ 200–400) over standardised day-ahead context (net position, wind fc, spot level and within-day position, hour, weekday, step), distance weighted by the LightGBM's split gains; the neighbours' empirical gap distribution **is** a CDF, so all eight columns read off it like the mixture. Blend with mixture/direct outputs per column, weights picked on folds A+B with bootstrap stability.
- **Evidence:** October's nearest month-analogue is **July** (down share 69.3 vs 74.7 %; spot std 10.5 vs 19.0) — the month recency weighting buries (Sep:Jul weight ≈ 3–6× at the proposed half-lives). Analogs are locally calibrated and cannot import September's volatility into a calm month. (QRF/distributional boosting share LightGBM's failure mode; a sequence model doesn't fit in 32 h — both skipped deliberately.)
- **Metrics:** MAE/pinball −1–2 % as an ensemble member; high insurance value if October breaks from September. Confidence medium as a hedge, medium-low alone.
- **Cost:** 3 h. **Leak traps:** library = training deliveries ≤ 4 Oct with their own D-1 context; nothing from the query origin that isn't usable there.
- **Go/no-go:** analog alone beats baseline MAE and pinball on fold A; blend beats both parents on A and B; else drop without regret.

### 3. Direction run-structure features
- **What:** from the usable lag-5 state: quarters since last direction change, current run length, previous run's direction; plus our own previous-origin direction forecast (deterministic, usable). Feeds π.
- **Evidence:** runs are short — up median 2 quarters (mean 4.3), down median 3 (mean 7.9); agreement decays 0.71 (lag 5) → 0.57 (lag 44) (round-2 facts).
- **Metrics:** direction Brier −0.005–0.01; mild pinball help at steps 1–12. Confidence medium. **Cost:** 1 h. **Leak traps:** none beyond the availability join.
- **Go/no-go:** gain on folds A and B. Note trailing up-share *alone* is worse than a constant (Brier 0.236/0.253 vs 0.224, `PLAN.md` dead ends) — it must earn its place as a feature, not a prior.

### 4. Demote Fingrid real-time from "the moat" to a gated option
- **What:** cap this workstream at 3 h behind two gates: (i) organisers confirm **in writing** the publication delay and that settled values proxy what was visible in real time; (ii) wind-error autocorrelation ≥ 0.5 at 1–10 h. If either fails by Sat noon, ship package-only + Open-Meteo.
- **Evidence:** the headline freshness prize is lag-1 (MAE 26.4), but an honest 30-min delay yields complete quarters only at **lag 3** (39.4) and newest samples at ~lag 2 (33.9) — and that is for the *gap itself*; wind error is only a proxy. The honest ceiling is a few €/MWh on steps 1–8. Meanwhile GLM's caveat (queries now return settled values; real-time visibility is unverifiable retroactively) makes this the plan's **largest code-review risk** — a challenged delay is a zero, not a bad metric.
- **Metrics:** protects all 80; small expected gain. Confidence high that risk/benefit is currently mispriced. **Cost:** −3 h net. **Leak traps:** that's the point.
- **Go/no-go:** gates above, plus a measured step-1–8 MAE gain on fold A before hour 4.

### 5. DST + submission-robustness suite with a frozen fallback
- **What:** tests around 25 Oct: exactly 673 origins × 40 steps; `delivery_start_utc` uniqueness; spot join on UTC (the spot file carries 8 repeated-hour rows, quality report); `cal_hour_local`/`cal_is_dst` handling of the duplicated local hour 3; trailing windows in UTC; final assertions (no NaN, p10 ≤ p50 ≤ p90, direction sum, spike sum ≤ 1); plus a **frozen, already-validated fallback CSV** that ships if the final run trips anything.
- **Evidence:** README §3 (673 origins, DST 25 Oct), validator §2.2. Several teams will lose their weekend here. **Metrics:** protects all 80. **Cost:** 1.5 h. **Go/no-go:** green before the first test-period run; no model change after the final validator pass.

**Single best idea, even though it's the red-team conclusion rather than a departure from my brief:** #1 — context-conditional calibration. It is the only lever aimed at the one thing we *know* about the test month, and it touches 50 automatic points where rival teams, using the round-1 consensus, will be mis-calibrated the same way we would be.

## 4. What to change or drop in the round-1 core

- **Recency weighting:** as proposed it upweights September 3–6× over July, the test month's nearest analogue. Ablate recency vs uniform vs context weights on fold B; keep only if it wins there too.
- **Calibration source:** last-3-Sep-weeks isotonic → idea #1 (keep the per-band structure, change what conditions it).
- **Additive quantile offsets → multiplicative widths around p50** — kills the sorting-after-selection problem (dragging p10 down to honour a foreign p50 worsens p10 pinball).
- **Per-column selection → per-triple** (p10/p50/p90 jointly) and per-pair (spikes), bootstrap-stable; direction probabilities always from one source, renormalised.
- **Demote:** Fingrid real-time (idea #4); VWAP/merit-order reconstruction (price = mFRR marginal in only 43 % of directional quarters and aFRR volumes are absent — keep activation-MW *features*, drop the reconstruction); UMM to dashboard-only.
- **Keep unchanged:** mixture CDF, direct challenger, Open-Meteo revisions (the freshest *legal* information for steps 5–40), constant p_none.
- **Drop:** trailing-state features as a pillar (dead-end evidence; one ablation at most); HMM smoothing (already out).

## 5. Three quick checks on the package data (≤ 30 min, before committing to #1/#2)

1. **Regime-analogue table.** Per local day (Jun 1–Oct 4): down share, spot std (join `fi_spot_price` on `timestamp_utc`), wind-fc mean, net-position mean; then nearest days to Oct 1–4. Confirms whether ≥ 10–15 October-like training days exist (support for #1/#2) and whether recency weighting is anti-adaptive. If the analogues are mostly July, fold B becomes the decisive fold.
2. **Calibration-map stability.** For a fixed direction model, compare per-month isotonic maps (or predicted mean p_up vs realised up share by month) and spike base rates by spot-vol tercile × net-position quintile. If Sep's map differs from Jun–Aug by > ~0.03 across the range, Sep-fitted calibration is regime-specific → adopt #1; the same bins define fold D.
3. **Down-gap width by month.** `gap.groupby([month, direction]).quantile([.05,.1,.25,.5,.75,.9,.95])` with gap = `fi_imbalance_price` − spot, direction = `fi_mfrr_direction`. If Sep's down P10/P90 is much wider than Jun–Aug's, Sep-fitted conformal overshoots in a 75 %-down October — the single biggest pinball risk (down gap is flat: −18 → −15 across volume quintiles). Also re-verify the 43 % mFRR-marginal equality to close the VWAP question.

All three use training data ≤ 4 Oct; spot used only through Oct 4 for diagnostics so nothing test-side enters any fitted object.

**Honest caveats:** my gain estimates are judgement calls stacked on measured drift, not measurements; the ~0.013 direction-Brier figure is an arithmetic bound, not a forecast; and if late October turns stormy rather than calm, #1's edge shrinks to spike/direction conditioning and the analog hedge earns its keep instead — which is why both are in the plan.
