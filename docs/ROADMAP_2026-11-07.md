## 4. P2 — bounded modelling experiments

Run only after R0/R1 are green. Keep the selected fallback unchanged. Change one factor at a time and save per-origin losses privately.

### R2.1 Calibration integrity — highest cross-metric value

**Configurations:** at most four predeclared controls:

1. current calibration;
2. 14 versus 28 days;
3. global versus three-band maps shrunk toward global;
4. frozen base+calibrator versus refit-and-carry.

**Metrics:** pinball, both Briers, MAE, coverage/width/miss rates by fold and horizon band.<br>
**Promote as verified:** consistent point improvement on both temporally separated folds, no material MAE failure, and paired moving-block-bootstrap 95% CIs excluding zero for the targeted improvement on both folds. Otherwise keep the result exploratory.<br>
**Kill:** fold disagreement, unstable rare-event maps, or gain confined to one week; an effect-size floor alone is not statistical evidence.<br>
**Do not:** tune isotonic/shrinkage repeatedly on either selection fold or call online adaptation legal without Q3.

### R2.2 Spike threshold-distance features

**Hypothesis:** distance from current gap/regulation state to the strict ±50 thresholds adds useful tail information.

- Use only as-of-safe features.
- Compare against identical direction/tail models without the new features.
- Evaluate spike Brier, direction Brier, and MAE jointly.

**Promote as verified:** positive spike-Brier improvement on both folds, no material MAE regression, and paired moving-block-bootstrap 95% CIs excluding zero on both folds. Treat **1% relative improvement only as an effect-size floor**, never as a significance claim.<br>
**Kill:** a clear fold reversal, an effect below the predeclared practical floor, or uncertainty too wide to support adoption; do not discard or promote from a point estimate alone.

### R2.3 Probability heads and `p_none`

- Compare logistic classifiers with L2/Brier-trained 0/1 heads using identical features/calibration.
- Compare direct spikes, direction-product spikes, and the same assembled probability after calibration.
- Keep `p_none` pooled or heavily shrunk; test horizon-band pooling only if event counts support it.

**Promote as verified:** rolling Brier point improvement on both folds with stable reliability and paired block-bootstrap 95% CIs excluding zero on both folds—not a single-month argmax hit-rate gain.<br>
**Kill:** base-rate collapse, fold inconsistency, degraded spike/direction coherence, or uncertainty too wide to distinguish the candidate from the incumbent.

### R2.4 Horizon-specific real-time state

- Preserve one global model as control.
- Test broad bands only; do not fit 40 independent models.
- Compare global versus early/late models under identical calibration.

**Adopt:** gains occur in the intended early band and aggregate metrics do not degrade.<br>
**Kill:** gains disappear outside one fold or later-horizon metrics worsen.

### R2.5 Coherent direction-conditional mixture — one structural bet

- Start with seven quantiles per direction and a small pooled none component.
- Derive P10/P50/P90 and both spike probabilities from the same CDF.
- Compare direct/product/CDF spike sources with common direction probabilities.
- Keep an explicitly labelled metric-wise recalibrated variant separate from the coherent version.

**Adopt:** rolling pinball/MAE/Brier gains without unstable tails.<br>
**Kill:** unstable interpolation/tails, inconsistent folds, or no gain over the simpler product model.

### R2.6 Ensemble freeze

- Prefer the existing fixed 50/50 blend over a large per-output weight search.
- If a stack is tested, use one nonnegative out-of-fold global weight per output or broad band.

**Adopt:** fold-mean improvement not carried by one week.<br>
**Kill:** another September-only weight search or any recipe whose components cannot be reproduced exactly.

### R2.7 Explicitly deprioritize

Cut in this order:

1. adaptive conformal/test-label recalibration without provider approval;
2. foundation-model or NGBoost searches;
3. regime-gated model selection;
4. additional horizon fragmentation;
5. new large architecture families.

## 5. Validation and statistical discipline

- Preserve the official September and August aggregates for comparability, but treat **both as selection-burned**: August also influenced the current two-fold ranking.
- Add rolling, temporally ordered evaluation blocks where data permit. **Refit the full training/calibration pipeline separately before each block**, admitting targets by `y_first_usable`; never evaluate a frozen model inside its own training window.
- Purge targets by actual publication availability, including forecast span and publication delay.
- Store per-origin contributions for all metrics and compare candidates with paired contiguous-origin moving-block bootstrap intervals; these overlapping origin-step rows are not independent observations.
- For the final integrated candidate versus incumbent, **predeclare the four official metric comparisons and apply a Holm correction or a joint block-bootstrap max-statistic**. Label every other improvement exploratory.
- Report fold/week/horizon consistency and event counts; rare-spike maps need pooling when counts are small.
- Prefer the simpler model when effects are fold-specific or uncertainty is too wide to distinguish candidates.
- Keep a negative-results log: recency weighting, import saturation, bid snapping, and other rejected ideas must not repeatedly re-enter through new branches.

## 6. P1/P2 — jury-facing Level 1 reasoning and Level 2 UI

These are high-value and independent of another regressor search.

### Required reasoning chain

Show the causal sequence clearly:

```text
weather/outage/load/network information
→ availability-safe system state and forecast errors
→ expected balancing direction
→ conditional gap/tail distribution
→ P10/P50/P90 + direction + spike probabilities
→ calibrated review suggestion, not autonomous dispatch
```

Include exact ablations, negative results, availability controls, external-source provenance, and hidden-test uncertainty.

### UI/demo priorities

1. **Honest time travel:** replay the exact forecast available at each selected origin, not hindsight-corrected output.
2. **Current + historical views:** next ten hours alongside previous day/week/month performance.
3. **Error-slicing panel:** horizon, direction, week, volatility, and data-freshness weaknesses.
4. **Regime/freshness explanation:** what changed and which information was available at the origin.
5. **Cost-aware alerts:** probability × consequence with configurable position/cost assumptions; do not invent plant economics.
6. **Conditional review suggestions:** “review market position/production plan,” with missing plant constraints stated; never “dispatch boiler/reserve” as an established capability.
7. **Consistency:** dashboard/API metrics and driver text must come from the same artifact as the report.

**Demo gate:** a fresh reviewer can reproduce the shown origin, see the data cutoff, distinguish demo/static data from live operation, and verify that all displayed metrics match the evidence manifest.
