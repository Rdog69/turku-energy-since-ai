## Production notes

**The two replay moments** (final model `split_all_rt_icon`, validation forecasts; export them with `python -m src.export.dashboard --model split_all_rt_icon --period validation`)
- **Hit, Fri 2 Oct 12:00 local** (origin 2026-10-02T09:00Z). Spike-high risk for 16:00–17:59 rose from 0.40 (11:00 origin) to 0.66. Top drivers in that block: the ECMWF-vs-ICON wind disagreement (10 of 12 quarters), the ICON wind revision and wind index. Realised gap 16:00–17:45: +38 to +125 €/MWh, all inside the P90 (+78 to +136). At 18:30–18:45 the gap (+205, +222) went past the band, so stop the replay at 18:00 or say so.
- **Miss, Tue 15 Sep 05:00 local** (origin 2026-09-15T02:00Z). P10–P90 gap about −105 to +50 for the whole morning, spike-high risk 0.02–0.09. Realised gap +276 at 06:00, +473 at 06:15, about +1,770 at 06:30–06:45. Inputs at the origin were between the 10th and 77th percentile of the validation period (wind error, frequency, live imbalance, plan error, ICON revision). Same event as Case 2 in `reports/case_studies.md`.
- Alert numbers: the alert rule in `reports/alerts_tuning.md` (block spike risk up at least 0.20 to at least 0.30) fires 2.2 times a day for the final model, and 61 % of alerts were followed by a high spike in that hour (base rate 19.5 %).
- ⚠ **Spot on screen.** Scenes 1–2 and the fan chart draw spot as a line. The repo rule is to never plot spot values (Nord Pool licence). Before publishing the video, either ask the organisers whether a spot line without axis values is allowed, or draw the gap to spot only.
- Both moments should be in the same viewing session, so the time-travel slider move in scene 5 is a single continuous drag.
- Every number read aloud comes from `docs/RESULTS.md` or the notes above (validation period). The organisers have not published test-period scores, so none are quoted.

**Rules to respect on screen**
- Never show a spot price *level* anywhere — not on axes, not in tooltips, not in the alert text. Show spot only as a line or as a gap in €/MWh. Blur or trim any dashboard tooltip that displays it.
- Everything in scenes 4–6 must be rendered from `results/dashboard/` output, not mocked up. If a panel isn't finished, cut it rather than fake it.
- Keep the "review" wording exactly as in the dashboard: review, check, consider. Never "sell", "buy", "reduce".

**Recording**
- Record at 1920×1080 with the browser at 100 % zoom; hide bookmarks bar and any Nord Pool tabs.
- One narrator voice, natural pace (~2.3 words/second as scripted). If a line runs long, cut adjectives, not content.
- Captions: burn in for scenes 1, 3, 7 (numbers and rules get misheard).
- Reserve 3 seconds of silence between scenes for the editor; total still fits under 5:00.
- Do a full timed dry run before recording. The dashboard replay (scene 4) is the one that overruns.
