# Surface: imbalance situation view (frontend/)

Mode: Operate. Audience: Turku Energia trading specialist (shift supervisor secondary). Job: see what is worth checking now and in the next 10 h, why, how sure, and how past forecasts did; replay any past hour. Constraints: local only (Nord Pool licence), EN/FI toggle, only data published by the review time.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The next ten hours drawn as a central-bank projection: one fan chart owns the screen and opens exactly at the review time. Refuses the dark terminal grid of green/red tiles and the SaaS card wall.

OWN-WORLD: Bank of Finland report: paper ground tinted toward blue-grey, deep institutional blue for the rail and headings, the fan in graded blues, vermilion only for high-price risk, sea-teal only for low-price risk, ink-grey tables with hairline rules. One workhorse grotesque with tabular figures; rank by weight and rule, few sizes. No shadows, no gradient chrome.

STORY: The trader reads in seconds whether the coming hours are calm, sees each flagged window with its drivers and probability, checks how the forecast has done over the last day and week, and drags time back to replay any hour as it looked then.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Blue left rail (name, language, period). Top strip: review time and the time slider. Centre, two-thirds width: fan chart (6 h realised, 10 h ahead), spot dotted, the plus and minus 50 EUR/MWh spike lines as hairlines, direction ribbon beneath. Right third: "Worth checking" list. Below the fold: quarter table and hindsight.
